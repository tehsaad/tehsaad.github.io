Solutions

Built for the way small shops actually sell.

Whether you sell from a phone, a counter or both, ShopPulse fits around how you already work.

Instagram and WhatsApp sellers

The usual problem

Orders arrive as DMs and voice notes. You copy details into your notes app and hope nothing gets lost between posting a reel and packing parcels.

How ShopPulse helps

  • Customer messages and orders live side by side
  • ReplyFlow answers the 'price?' and 'size available?' questions with your real stock
  • Cash on delivery tracked until the money is in

Clothing stores and boutiques

The usual problem

Every design comes in four sizes and three colours. Knowing that the medium in olive is sold out shouldn't need a stock count.

How ShopPulse helps

  • Stock tracked per size and colour
  • Low-stock alerts before the bestseller runs out
  • Walk-in and online sales counted in the same place

Accessories and jewellery brands

The usual problem

Lots of small items, low prices, and customers who buy several at once. Margins disappear if you don't know what actually sells.

How ShopPulse helps

  • See margins with cost prices that customers never see
  • Best sellers and slow movers by revenue and units
  • Multi-item orders with discounts and free delivery thresholds

Local shops with a counter

The usual problem

You know your regulars by face, but not what they bought last time or how much they spend with you.

How ShopPulse helps

  • Record a walk-in sale in a few taps
  • Customer profiles with history and notes
  • Staff accounts, so you're not the only one who knows everything
