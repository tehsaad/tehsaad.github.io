Features

Everything a small shop needs. Nothing it doesn't.

ShopPulse covers the daily work of running a shop: stock, orders, customers, messages and the numbers behind them.

  • Inventory
  • Orders
  • Customers
  • ReplyFlow

Inventory

Know what's on the shelf without counting it.

Products with sizes and colours, each with its own stock count. Sales take stock off automatically, and deliveries, returns and damaged items are recorded with a reason.

  • Variants by size, colour or both
  • Low-stock alerts at a level you choose
  • Stock movement history for every product
  • Search, filter and sort by stock level, category or price

Orders

Every order, wherever it came from.

Record orders from Instagram, WhatsApp, your website or the counter. Each one moves through clear steps, so anyone on your team can see what's next.

  • Pending, confirmed, preparing, ready, completed
  • A timeline of who changed what, and when
  • Cash on delivery, bank transfer, card and cash
  • Delivery fees and discounts worked out for you

Customers

Remember every customer, even on a busy day.

A profile for each person with their orders, conversations, total spend and notes, so returning customers feel recognised.

  • Order history and lifetime spend
  • New, returning, VIP and inactive at a glance
  • Private team notes
  • All their conversations in one place

ReplyFlow

Faster replies that are actually correct.

ReplyFlow reads the question, checks your stock, prices and shop notes, and drafts a reply. You send it, change it, or write your own. It never sends anything by itself.

  • Suggested replies grounded in your real data
  • Shows what each suggestion is based on
  • Messages are marked when AI helped write them
  • Take over any conversation and suggestions pause

Analytics

Numbers that tell you what to do next.

Revenue, orders, best sellers, repeat customers and stock movement, over 7, 30 or 90 days. Each chart answers a question a shop owner actually asks.

  • Revenue and order trends
  • Top products and sales channels
  • Customer growth and average order value
  • Stock received versus sold
