Agreement

By creating a ShopPulse account, you agree to these terms on behalf of yourself and your shop.

Your account

  • Keep your login details safe. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • You can invite team members, and you are responsible for what they do in your shop.

Plans and billing

Paid plans are billed monthly in Pakistani Rupees. Trials last 14 days. We give at least 30 days' notice before changing the price of your plan. You can cancel at any time; your plan stays active until the end of the month you've paid for.

Acceptable use

Don't use ShopPulse to sell illegal goods, send spam, or misuse your customers' information. We may suspend accounts that do.

Your data

You own the data you put into ShopPulse. We use it only to run the service for you, as described in our privacy policy.

AI-assisted features

ReplyFlow suggestions can be wrong. Check them before sending. You are responsible for messages you choose to send to your customers.

Ending the agreement

You can close your account at any time. We may close accounts that break these terms, and will give notice where we reasonably can.
