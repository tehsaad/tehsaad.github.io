About

Software that respects how small shops work.

ShopPulse is an early-stage product. We're building it for the sellers who run their whole business from a phone, a notebook and a lot of DMs.

Why ShopPulse exists

Small shops are some of the hardest-working businesses there are. They sell across Instagram, WhatsApp and a counter at the same time, and they keep track of it all in their heads.

Most business software is built for bigger companies, with bigger budgets and someone whose job is to set it up. Small sellers end up with spreadsheets that nobody updates, or nothing at all.

ShopPulse is our attempt at the tool we think they deserve: one place for stock, orders, customers and conversations, simple enough to use from day one, with AI that helps without taking over.

What we won't compromise on

  • Simple first

    If a shop owner needs a tutorial to record an order, we've built it wrong. Every screen should make sense on a busy day, on a phone.

  • Your data is yours

    Your products, customers and orders belong to you. You'll always be able to export them, and we'll never sell them.

  • AI helps, people decide

    ReplyFlow drafts replies, but a person always sends them. Anything AI helped write is labelled, so nobody is misled.

  • Built for Pakistan first

    Rupees, cash on delivery, WhatsApp orders and Instagram shops are the starting point, not an afterthought.

Where we are today

ShopPulse is in early access. The core of the product works, and you can try it with a demo shop. Some features, like Instagram and WhatsApp connections, are still being built. We mark those clearly as “coming soon” wherever they appear.
