The problem

Selling is the easy part. Keeping track is what wears you out.

Most small shops run on memory, screenshots and a notebook. It works, until it gets busy.

  1. 01

    Orders live in five different chats

    An Instagram DM here, a WhatsApp voice note there, a walk-in you meant to write down. Something always slips.

    Stock is a guess until it isn't

    You find out the medium is sold out when a customer has already paid, or when you're packing the parcel.

    The person who's ordered six times gets the same reply as a stranger, because nobody remembers.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

Something else on your mind? Send us a message.

Do I need to be good with computers to use ShopPulse?

No. If you can use Instagram and WhatsApp, you can use ShopPulse. Adding a product takes about a minute, and everything works on your phone.

Does the AI reply to my customers on its own?

No. ReplyFlow drafts a reply using your real stock and prices, and you decide whether to send it, edit it or write your own. Nothing is sent without you pressing Send.

Can I use it if I only sell on Instagram?

Yes. Many sellers start with one channel. You can record orders from Instagram, WhatsApp, your website or walk-in customers in the same place.

Which currencies are supported?

ShopPulse uses Pakistani Rupees (PKR) by default. Support for other currencies is planned for shops that sell abroad.

What happens to my data if I stop using ShopPulse?

Your data belongs to you. You'll be able to export your products, orders and customers as a spreadsheet at any time.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free for small catalogues. Paid plans have a 14-day trial, and you don't need a card to start.
